HotDeskBabe

HotDeskBabe is a private software assistant operated by a single household. It is not a public service and is not distributed to other users.

The application connects to one private Google account to read shared family calendars and compile a short nightly schedule briefing. It also organizes household documents on the operator's own computer.

It collects no analytics, runs no advertising, and shares data with no third parties.

The account owner can revoke the application's access at any time from the Google Account security page.
