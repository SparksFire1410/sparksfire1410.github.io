HotDeskBabe Privacy Policy

HotDeskBabe is a private, single-household application. This policy explains what it can access and how that access is used.

Access: when authorized, the application can read the Google Calendar, Drive, Contacts (read-only), Sheets, and Docs of the single private Google account it is connected to. It has no email access.

Use: calendar and document data is processed locally on the operator's own computer, solely to prepare family schedule briefings and organize the household's own records.

Sharing: no data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. No analytics are collected.

Control: the account owner can revoke the application's access at any time via Google Account → Security → Third-party access.
